Highwater QBO App

An internal automation tool that posts journal entries and reads reports from QuickBooks Online for Highwater Cafe & Market and Kismet's Landing.

About this app

This is a private, single-operator automation tool built on Intuit's official QuickBooks Online API. It runs locally on the operator's computer as a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. It is not distributed publicly and is not available for use by other parties.

What it does

  • Posts daily-sales journal entries from POS reports
  • Posts payroll journal entries from payroll exports
  • Reads financial reports (P&L, balance sheet, account lists)
