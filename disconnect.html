Disconnecting the Highwater QBO App

Last updated: May 20, 2026

The Highwater QBO App is an internal, single-operator automation tool. It runs locally on the Operator's own computer and holds an OAuth refresh token for each connected QuickBooks Online company.

To disconnect the app from a QuickBooks Online company:

  1. Sign in to qbo.intuit.com with the QBO login for that company.
  2. Open Settings (gear icon) → Manage users → Apps, or browse to https://app.qbo.intuit.com/app/managemyapps.
  3. Locate the “Kismet” app in the list of connected apps.
  4. Click Disconnect. The refresh token held by the local app will immediately become invalid.
  5. On the Operator's computer, remove the corresponding refresh token from the local environment file.

After disconnection, the app can no longer call the QuickBooks Online API for that company until it is re-connected through the OAuth flow described on the Connect page.
